## Production Tips for Canva & Motion Designers

1. **Aspect Ratio**: Set canvas to `1080 x 1920 px` (9:16 vertical).
2. **Safe Zones**: Keep key text and visual focal points within the vertical safe area (top 15% and bottom 20% reserved for platform UI overlays).
3. **Pacing**: Use quick cuts (every 2–3 seconds) and smooth slide-in/fade-in animations for captions.
4. **Color Palette**:
   - Dark background: `#0f172a` (Slate 900)
   - Accents: Electric Cyan (`#06b6d4`), Neon Green (`#22c55e`), Vibrant Purple (`#8b5cf6`)
5. **Soundtrack**: Upbeat, energetic royalty-free electronic / tech beat (120–128 BPM).
