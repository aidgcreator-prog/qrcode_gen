# Privacy Policy for QRCodeGen

**Last Updated:** September 14, 2026  
**Publisher:** LocalAiLab  
**Product:** QRCodeGen (Microsoft Store ID: 9NJPWQXHKNWD)

---

## 1. Overview

LocalAiLab built **QRCodeGen** as an offline-first desktop utility for Windows. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data and personal information.

We believe your data belongs to you. **QRCodeGen does not collect, store, track, sell, or transmit any personal data or user inputs.**

---

## 2. Personal Information & Data Collection

- **No Personal Information Collected:** QRCodeGen does not request, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, IP address, physical location, or device identifiers.
- **No Telemetry or Tracking:** The application does not include any third-party analytics, diagnostic telemetry, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies.
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, log in, or provide any credentials to use any feature of QRCodeGen.

---

## 3. QR Code Generation & Local Processing

- **100% Local Generation:** All QR code generation and image rendering (PNG, JPEG, SVG) take place completely on your local computer.
- **No Data Transmission:** The text, URLs, credentials, or other inputs you enter to generate QR codes are processed in-memory on your device and are **never** transmitted across the internet or shared with external servers.

---

## 4. File Storage & System Access

- **Local Storage Only:** Generated QR codes are saved exclusively to your local device (in the local `outputs/` folder or your user `Pictures\QR Codes` folder).
- **No Cloud Sync:** QRCodeGen does not upload or synchronize your saved images to any cloud storage or remote servers.
- **Minimal Permissions:** The application only accesses disk storage necessary to write the images you choose to export and to maintain your local application preferences.

---

## 5. Network Access & Update Checks

- The core QR code generator operates entirely offline without requiring an internet connection.
- In desktop environments, the application may check the public GitHub Releases API (`https://api.github.com/repos/aidgcreator-prog/qrcode_gen/releases/latest`) solely to notify you when an updated version is available. No user data, device telemetry, or personal information is sent during this check.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

QRCodeGen does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published directly in this repository.
